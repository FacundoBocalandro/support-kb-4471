The fix queued for #4471 changes the shared parser. It does not close the other two tickets.

The three problems

parser: the parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A date like 7/4 comes out as 4 July, not 7 April. The report shows the day and the month swapped. This comes in through the app, by email and through booking-v2 forms.

hint: the booking-v3 form shows the wrong hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type the date month first, as the hint tells them. Then the form reads the date day first. This is a different bug from the parser, so the parser fix does not close these reports. Only reports from the form channel at booking-v3 tenants have this problem.

shift: the date moves by one day. These reports do not show a swap. The date moves one day back or forward: Tuesday shows as Monday, two clients in different time zones get different days, and an invite arrives before the booking. This looks like a time zone conversion bug. The desk listed these reports in its notes, but the #4471 table still counts them under parser.

How the reports were placed

  1. Remove the reports that the export repeats. One id is one report.
  2. Put each report that the desk read as a one-day move under shift.
  3. Place every other report by its tenant: its locale and its booking form (causes.ts). Do not use the intake tag. Intake guesses the tag from the browser of the reporter, and the “intake tag wrong” column shows how often that guess is wrong.

Why one ticket held three problems

Decision 0001 merges reports when their subject lines match. The same subject lines come in for all three problems, for example “Wrong day on the invite”, so subject matching put all of them in #4471. A subject tells you what the reporter saw. It does not tell you the cause.
